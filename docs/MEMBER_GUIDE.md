# Member guide

Welcome to the ELCSA Tshwane City Parish app. You can use it to keep your details
up to date, register your children, request baptism or confirmation, download your
certificates, and see what's on in the parish.

## 1. Get the app on your phone

You don't need an app store. Open the parish website in your phone's browser, then:

- **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install the app**. If you don't see the button, open the browser menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The app's icon is now on your home screen like any other app. (On an iPhone you may
need to sign in again the first time you open the installed app.)

## 2. Create your account

1. On the first screen choose **I'm a Member**, then **Create Account**.
2. Follow the steps: **Your Congregation** → **About You** (your name and ward) → **Sacraments & League** (tell us if you're already baptised, confirmed or in a league) → **Create Your Login** (your email and a password of **at least 8 characters**).
3. When it says your account is created, sign in.

Your email is your username. You don't need to confirm it by email.

> **Forgot your password?** The app can't send emails, so there is no reset link. Ask the parish office and they'll give you a temporary password.

## 3. Your portal (My Portal)

When you sign in you land on **My Portal**. At the top you'll see your **Ward**, **League**, **Baptism** and **Confirmation**.
Once something is recorded you can tap **View Certificate**.

Three buttons switch what's below: **Dashboard**, **Leagues** and **Calendar**.

### My Details
Add or change your **phone**, **birthday**, **gender** and **profession**. Your profession is optional; if you add it, you appear in the member **Directory**
(see the privacy section below).

### Update Your Involvement
Want to be baptised or confirmed, or join (or change) a league? Choose it and send a request:

- **Request Baptism** — say whether it's an infant or adult baptism and who the sponsor / godparent is.
- **Request Confirmation** — you can add your mentor and a photo of your baptism certificate.
- **Request Change** (league) — tell us why you'd like to join; you can attach certificate photos.

The parish office will look at your request. If they approve it they'll **suggest a date**. You then see a message asking
**"Yes, that works"** or **"Needs a different date"**. Once you accept, it's done and added to the church calendar.

### Family and household
- **Family** — if a relative has already registered, **Join a family** by entering their **family code** or by **searching their name**. Choose **No, I'm on my own** if that's you.
- **My Household** — add your children who don't have a phone with **Add Child**. You can then manage their details, and request baptism, confirmation or a league for them. Tap **Manage** on a child to open them.
- You can rename your family or remove a member from it.

## 4. Certificates

Tap **View Certificate** under Baptism, Confirmation or League once it's recorded. You'll see the certificate with a Bible verse.

- **On the website / computer:** press **Download Certificate** to save a picture.
- **On your phone:** press **Save / Share Certificate** to save it or send it, for example on WhatsApp.

If something on a certificate is wrong (a spelling, a date, a parent's name), tell the parish office — only they can correct it.

## 5. Everything else

- **Parish Announcements** — what's coming up. Tap **Listen** to hear one read aloud.
- **Calendar** — the church year, parish events and **upcoming birthdays**. On your own birthday the app greets you!
- **Directory** — members who've shared their profession, with their ward and phone number. Tap a person to call them.
- **Banking** — the parish's bank details and the **reference code** to put on your deposit (tap the copy icon to copy). If giving by SnapScan is set up you can also pay through the app.
- **Once a year** the app asks whether you're still part of the parish. Answer **Yes, still here** or **I've left**.
- Sign out with the icon at the top right.

## 6. Your information and privacy

The app keeps only what the parish needs to run: your details, your family, sacraments and certificates, and your giving through
SnapScan. Other signed-in members can see an upcoming-birthdays list (name and birthday) and, only if you've shared a profession,
your directory entry. Nothing is sold or used for advertising, and the app uses no analytics.

You can read the full policy in the app — **Privacy Policy** on the first screen and the sign-in screen — and ask the parish office to see, correct or
delete what they hold about you.

## Something not working?

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| Can't sign in | Check the email and password; if you've forgotten it, ask the parish office. |
| The app looks old or something is missing | Close it completely and open it again. |
| The screen won't load or says it failed to fetch | Check your data or Wi-Fi. If it still fails, the parish office may need to wake the system. |
| A request or certificate is wrong | Contact the parish office — 959 Pretorius Street, Arcadia, Tshwane. |
